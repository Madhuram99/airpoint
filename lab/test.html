Pointing test

Nine circles sit on a ring. Click the lit circle; the next one lights up across the ring. Go as fast as you can while still landing inside. A click outside counts as a miss and the test moves on.

There are three levels: big targets close together, then smaller and further apart. Each level starts when you click the top circle.
