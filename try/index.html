A taste of AirPoint, the open-source hand-sign mouse for people who cannot use one. The full version adds tremor filtering, personal calibration, signs you teach it, a face mode and more. Nothing is uploaded: the hand tracking runs in this page. self-test

Move a cursor with your hand

Hold your open hand up to the camera, about half an arm away. An open palm moves the cursor. Pinch your thumb and finger together to click.

The camera picture never leaves this device: it is read in this page and thrown away. The page loads its hand-tracking library from a public CDN.
